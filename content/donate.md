## Standing Up for Our Neighborhood

Neighbors of 2411 is a grassroots group of residents in Orange, CA fighting to stop a 24/7 industrial warehouse from being built at 2411 N. Glassell St, just feet from homes and blocks from Fletcher Mandarin Language and GATE Academy. We're not against progress; we're asking the City to take a hard look at the noise, diesel truck traffic, and air quality impacts this project would bring to our families before it's approved.

This fight is won with facts. Your support helps us retain independent technical experts to review the City's environmental analysis, cover filing and administrative costs as we pursue every available appeal, and keep our neighbors informed at every stage of this process.
